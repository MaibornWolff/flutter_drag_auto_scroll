## 0.1.0

- Initial release.
- `DragAutoScroller` -- wraps scrollable content with edge-zone auto-scrolling.
- `AutoScrollDraggable<T>` -- drop-in `Draggable` replacement that signals drag state.
- `DragAutoScrollController` -- shared controller for cross-tree scenarios.
- `DragAutoScrollScope` -- `InheritedWidget` for same-tree lookup.
- Edge zone overlays with configurable color and threshold.
